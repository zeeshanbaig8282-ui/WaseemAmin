About

24+ years · 19+ in oil and gas

I am a civil engineer who moved from buildings, canals and dams into subsea structures. Since 2015 I have led structural and tie-in engineering on the owner's side at EniProgetti, on large West African and Mozambique developments.

The work runs from bid and design review through fabrication, factory acceptance tests and offshore installation. I keep a lessons-learned register on every project, coordinate with other disciplines when scope shifts, and train new staff.

Earlier, at INTECSEA, Woodgroup Kenny, Saipem and 2H Offshore in the UK, I did the detailed design and analysis of the structures I now review.
